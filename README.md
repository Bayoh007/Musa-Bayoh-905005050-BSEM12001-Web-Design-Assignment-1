EcoTrack SL

EcoTrack SL is a web-based platform designed to support **Sustainable Development
Goal 12 (Responsible Consumption and Production)** by helping citizens report waste issues, learn recycling practices,
and track environmental impact in Sierra Leone.

Project Overview
EcoTrack SL addresses the problem of poor waste management by providing a simple and accessible digital solution.
The platform connects citizens with local authorities and promotes sustainable environmental practices.


Objectives
- Improve waste reporting in local communities  
- Promote awareness of recycling and proper waste disposal  
- Track environmental impact and community participation  
- Support sustainable development initiatives  

 Features

- *Report Waste  
  Users can take photos and upload locations of waste issues.

Recycling Tips 
  Provides simple guides on how to reduce, reuse, and recycle.

Impact Tracking 
  Displays statistics such as number of reports, areas cleaned, and active users.

-Location-Based Reporting**  
  Helps authorities identify and respond to waste problems quickly.

 Responsive Design Approach

The platform follows a **mobile-first design strategy**:
- Content stacks vertically on smaller devices  
- Navigation is simplified using a menu icon  
- Buttons are optimized for touch interaction  
- Layout adapts to ensure readability across all devices

## 🧠 Design Decisions

- Minimalist layout to focus on usability  
- Clear call-to-action buttons to guide users  
- Simple navigation for easy access  
- Mobile-first approach due to high mobile usage

By encouraging proper waste disposal and promoting recycling awareness, EcoTrack SL contributes to a cleaner and more sustainable environment.
